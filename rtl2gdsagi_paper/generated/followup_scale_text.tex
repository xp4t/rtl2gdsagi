\subsection{Live repair of larger failed implementations}
Four additional episodes start from the retained accumulator spacing
and antenna configurations, with two declared repeats per configuration.
The design, \code{accum\_bank32}, contains 32 eight-bit accumulator
lanes and a read-selection multiplexer. The antenna configuration selects
passive fillers; the spacing configuration retains default fillers.
Table~\ref{tab:largerepair} lists every episode.
Of four episodes, 0 complete the full contract, 2 end in policy
escalation and 2 are interrupted by insufficient provider credit.
They record 23 valid model responses and 2 rejected provider
requests. Each first encounters an actual native
failure. These exploratory episodes retain all outcomes and are reported
separately from the original injected-fault cohort. The initial failure,
every authorized update and native recheck, provider errors and final
candidate or failure state are retained in the follow-up catalog.
Provider interruptions are not interpreted as algorithmic repair failures.
Policy escalation denotes the controller stopping when a proposed
remediation would repeat previously failed stage inputs and configuration.

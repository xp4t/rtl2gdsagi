1   Source and Execution Ledger

The primary manuscript uses frozen implementation af31b39f6782 for four-
teen design runs, twenty separate recorded-proposal executions, eighteen fault-
consistency executions, and a replayed spacing-repair episode. The latter submits
the recorded padding delta through a scripted proposal adapter and makes zero
new model calls. It encounters native spacing failure, authorizes the recorded
change, reruns the affected stages, and certifies. It supplies the post-repair arti-
fact used by the new swap fixture. The 24 scripted baseline candidates use the
same frozen native executor plus the hashed first-failure stopping harness de-
scribed in the sweep protocol below. Both documents use Springer LNCS. The
archive includes a portable hash verifier; native reruns require the pinned stack.
Review bindings and publication receipts record separate checks.
    The twenty recorded proposals originate from live Claude Sonnet 5 runs (An-
thropic; identifier claude-sonnet-5) on snapshot 18d948815ac6. The primary
replay counts those proposal states, with six frozen certifications and fourteen
native failures. It is separate from the original nine live episodes, which produced
seven certifications and two escalations on that earlier code. Source snapshots,
complete typed configurations, and commands identify both phases. Current
source is af31b39f6782; Git commits modifying the manuscript and drivers do
not introduce a third implementation snapshot.
    The 2x2x2 padding ablation retains four source interventions derived from
the frozen source. Their changes remove only the CTS and/or routing padding
calls. Worker count is the other declared factor. The mapped netlist and placed
DEF match across the CTS intervention, while CTS and downstream artifacts
differ. The main frozen renderer retains both padding calls. Table 1 gives all
eight outcomes.

4   Scripted Sweep and Stopping Policy
The 24-state sweep derives initial configurations before the first failed na-
tive check of each recorded fixture. It applies fixed numerical choices, never
model responses: padding 0–3, offset 50/2 µm, and the full sixteen-state DE-
LAY0/DELAY1, density 0.45/0.55, padding 1/2, setup-repair off/on grid at 3
ns. CTS optimization margins stay at 0.05 ns; the acceptance guardband stays
zero. Two period controls use the initial AREA0 timing state at 3.25 and 3.50
ns. They keep delay fractions 0.2, which changes absolute input/output delay

from 0.60 ns to 0.65/0.70 ns; uncertainty and load remain fixed. These are
combined requirement changes, excluded from fixed-target repair counts. All
eight setup off/on pairs yield identical CTS/routed DEF, SPEF and setup/hold
WNS. Diagnostic re-execution of the eight setup-on CTS scripts retains their
inputs and command order, adding only slack markers and redirected out-
put snapshots. Every setup call is entered and returns normally. Setup WNS
immediately before and after the call is unchanged at 194.356–274.527 ps,
above the 50 ps target. The before/after DEF and netlist hashes match, and
every final diagnostic CTS DEF matches its original candidate. The scripts set
clock-layer RC but omit signal-wire RC; native warnings RSZ-0014/RSZ-0021
report zero wire capacitance and wire-load fallback. Thus no setup violation
is visible at the repair point under that estimator, while later routed SPEF
timing fails. This limits what the toggle experiment establishes about setup
repair. The eight logs, scripts, markers and snapshot hashes are retained in
runs/setup_repair_diagnostic_20261013; these are diagnostics, not addi-
tional end-to-end candidates or certifications.

5   Guardband Sensitivity from Retained Slacks

The acceptance guardband in every recorded run is g = 0. We calculate eligi-
bility at g = 0.05 ns without modifying implementation, constraints, reports or
certificates. Post-CTS hold and routed setup/hold must all reach that threshold;
every other obligation retains its recorded outcome. Table 3 reports the seven
primary designs and the archived narrow timing repair. All fourteen primary
runs pass at g = 0; twelve (six designs) remain eligible at 50 ps. Both shift1024
repeats fail routed hold, with 49.477 ps against 50 ps; post-CTS hold still passes.
The archived +1.586 ps setup repair fails the stricter setup threshold.

    The spacing-stop episode’s last proposal raises padding from four to six sites.
A previous six-site attempt at the same density already failed placement with
DPL-0036. The sequence cycles through padding two, four and six; the recorded
earlier DRC counts are one and four markers before the six-site placement fail-
ure. The controller rejects revisiting that failed placement fingerprint after 7/24
diagnosis attempts.
    The antenna-stop episode first increases repair iterations to ten and the pre-
diction margin to 40%, then changes maximum routing layer to six and the
detailed-router iteration limit to 64. Layer six causes GRT-0005. The final pro-
posal restores maximum layer five. The resulting antenna-stage inputs revisit
the prior failed signature, which had three violating nets/pins, and the controller
stops after 3/24 diagnosis attempts. These are revisited failed states rather than
global-budget exhaustion or an unchanged literal final delta.
    Earlier spacing, antenna and diode-legalization probes of the accumulator,
ALU banks, and other scale candidates remain in the native archive. They are
excluded from the seven clean primary designs. Exact mapped-cell counts, failed
stages, and intermediate observations are preserved in the JSON catalog.

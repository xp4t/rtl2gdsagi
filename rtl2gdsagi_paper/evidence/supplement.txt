1   Source and Execution Ledger

The primary manuscript uses frozen implementation af31b39f6782 for four-
teen design runs, twenty separate recorded-proposal executions, eighteen fault-
consistency executions, and a replayed spacing-repair episode. The latter submits
the recorded padding delta through a scripted proposal adapter and makes zero
new model calls. It encounters native spacing failure, authorizes the recorded
change, reruns the affected stages, and certifies. It supplies the post-repair arti-
fact used by the new swap fixture.
    The twenty recorded proposals originate from live Claude Sonnet 5 runs (An-
thropic; identifier claude-sonnet-5) on snapshot 18d948815ac6. The primary
replay counts those proposal states, with six frozen certifications and fourteen
native failures. It is separate from the original nine live episodes, which produced
seven certifications and two escalations on that earlier code. Source snapshots,
complete typed configurations, and commands identify both phases. Current
source is af31b39f6782; Git commits modifying the manuscript and drivers do
not introduce a third implementation snapshot.
    The 2x2x2 padding ablation retains four source interventions derived from
the frozen source. Their changes remove only the CTS and/or routing padding
calls. Worker count is the other declared factor. The mapped netlist and placed
DEF match across the CTS intervention, while CTS and downstream artifacts
differ. The main frozen renderer retains both padding calls.

3   Complete-State Replay Reconstruction
Reconstruction starts with the archived IR defaults and applies the logged char-
acterization seed, initial overrides, curated numerical changes, and all accepted
model deltas in temporal order. The archived renderer used the native one-
worker default; the current replay declares that effective count explicitly. The
reconstructed final IR matches every archived successful episode before any new
native execution. Fresh runs disable further repair.
    An initial reconstruction pilot omitted the curated PDN changes preceding
the model call: strap offset 50 to 21.42 µm and utilization 0.35 to 0.29. Its three
PDN configurations did not represent the recorded promoted states. The com-
plete pilot and original launcher remain in the archive and are excluded from the
final twenty-state result. The corrected cohort was declared and executed sepa-
rately, with the full-state equality checks described above. This exclusion corrects
configuration reconstruction rather than selecting favorable native outcomes.

5    Larger Episodes and Stopping Proposals
The 1,995-cell accum_bank32 contains thirty-two eight-bit accumulator lanes
and a read-selection multiplexer. Two spacing and two antenna episodes were
declared on af31b39f6782. Table 3 retains all four: none completes certifica-
tion, two stop on previously failed fingerprints, and two remain incomplete. The
latter outcomes stay unknown; their saved candidate states are not certified or
labeled eventual algorithmic repair failures. Twenty-three valid model responses
are retained across this separate cohort. It is not pooled with the original nine
episodes.

      Abstract. RTL2GDSAGI binds native verification evidence to the im-
      plementation selected for release. With frozen controller af31b39f6782,
      seven SKY130 designs spanning 16–1024 mapped cells complete the
      21-stage verification contract twice. All seven pairs reproduce routed
      DEF, mapped and routed netlists, extracted parasitics, and date-
      normalized GDS; the 1,024-cell shift register uses one routing worker.
      Six constructed classes of stale or incomplete evidence are blocked in
      three consistency executions each. In the archived timing trajectories
      from snapshot 18d948815ac6, 11/14 model proposals fail native checks,
      two lack completed target checks, and one passes. Frozen-source replay
      fails 14/14 timing states, but this comparison is confounded by a CTS-
      padding source change that reverses the archived pass. These proposal
      states were collected under iterative native feedback. A scripted baseline
      certifies spacing and PDN configurations without a model; 0/16 timing
      configurations certify at unchanged 3 ns. The nine live repair episodes
      are a recorded case study from snapshot 18d948815ac6; live repair was
      not re-executed on the frozen source.

      Keywords: RTL-to-GDS · physical verification · artifact provenance ·
      electronic design automation



1   Introduction

OpenROAD and OpenLane provide open synthesis and physical-implementation
infrastructure [1,2]. Release combines geometric, connectivity and timing evi-
dence bound to the selected implementation and verification inputs. A zero-
marker report may concern another GDS or omit required rules; repair can
invalidate earlier evidence.
    RTL2GDSAGI records artifact identity, consumed inputs, parser contracts
and verdicts, with typed proposal authorization and native rechecking. We ask
which constructed evidence failures are blocked, which recorded proposal states
survive native checks, and which artifacts reproduce on a frozen controller. The
contribution is this executable acceptance contract and its measured integration
with existing native EDA algorithms.

2      Evidence Contract

2.1     Candidate identity and release checks

The controller executes characterization, lint, simulation, constraints, synthesis,
synthesis equivalence, pre-layout timing, floorplan, power-network construction,
placement, clock-tree synthesis (CTS), post-CTS timing, routing, extraction,
post-route timing, GDS stream-out, DRC, LVS, antenna checking, routed equiv-
alence, and signoff. Pre-layout timing is advisory; post-CTS timing gates hold
slack, and post-route timing gates setup and hold. Release requires all declared
hard obligations to have positive evidence.
    The artifact ledger stores each output’s producer, path, size, and SHA-256
hash. Directory hashing uses relative filenames and contents. Writing H for
SHA-256, the candidate identity is

                  C = H(T, {(k, H(Ak ))}k∈B , P, V, H(K), H(I)) ,                    (1)

where T is the top, B the bound artifacts, P the recorded process-design kit
(PDK) identity, V the tool identities, K the run configuration, and I the typed
configuration state. P includes name, root, cell library, and corner. The layer
map and verification decks are hashed members of B; the whole installed PDK
is not content-hashed.
    A gate records consumed and produced artifact hashes, report identity, parser
contract, and accepted tool identity. Release revalidation rehashes files, checks
lineage, and requires DRC and LVS to consume the selected GDS. Native KLay-
out arguments are checked against the selected input and deck bytes. The certifi-
cation identifier commits to the candidate and gate records. Figure 1 summarizes
the decision boundary.

2.2   Native obligations and repair
DRC requires a usable report, the expected top, and the approved active-rule in-
ventory under the exact deck. This evaluation uses 211 normalized categories; 213
raw declarations include two approved aliases. A zero-marker report with missing
categories fails the inventory check. LVS requires agreement among process sta-
tus, log verdict, and a native database with actual circuit cross-references, plus an
approved deck containing executable compare. Stream-out requires nonempty ge-
ometry, resolved references, and mapped layers. The power distribution network
(PDN) check requires supply nets, rails, straps, and vertical via connections.
    Extraction compares logical net identities in Standard Parasitic Exchange
Format (SPEF) and routed Design Exchange Format (DEF), rejects missing or
duplicate nets, and requires complete parasitic sections. Native static timing
analysis (STA) separately requires finite setup/hold slack and zero unannotated
or partially unannotated drivers. Clock period, uncertainty, output load, RTL
function, and verification decks are protected from physical-only repairs. The
slack guardband g = sta.slack_guardband_ns ≥ 0 is the minimum required
slack: post-CTS requires hold WNS ≥ g, and routed STA requires both setup
and hold WNS ≥ g. All evaluated runs use g = 0 ns. This accepts nonnegative
slack without an additional robustness margin; the 0.25 ns clock uncertainty
is a separate timing constraint. Neither a small positive slack nor exact repeat
hashes establish margin against variation.
    A typed change rolls execution back to its earliest affected stage and invali-
dates downstream evidence. The recorded live episodes configure three attempts
per stage and 24 diagnosis attempts. These settings do not establish universal
termination. The controller stops when a proposal would revisit a previously
failed stage fingerprint, including its inputs and effective configuration. Formal
equivalence checks both synthesis and routed netlists using EQY [3]. Scalar-net
matching uses splitnets on; grouping related signals under [collect *] and
group * preserves coupled-state proof structure. Fresh counter and shift-register
controls prove equivalent, while a retained counter mutant with a forced flop fails
equivalence.

3.2    Coverage and fixed-proposal replay

The coverage unit is one of six author-constructed fault classes: stale same-top
GDS, missing front-end-of-line (FEOL) rule categories, LVS without compare,
missing PDN vias, missing layer map, and artifact substitution after a verified
repair. Three executions per class check consistent behavior. Fixtures run native
tools where applicable and exercise production parsers, structural checks, invo-
cation checks, or gate lineage. Their selection against declared obligations makes
this coverage testing, with no population false-pass-rate estimate.
    The recorded proposals come from nine live Claude Sonnet 5 (Anthropic,
claude-sonnet-5) episodes: three each for ALU spacing, register PDN con-
struction, and ALU setup timing. Replay reconstructs complete effective config-
uration states, including characterization seeds, curated changes that preceded
model calls, and cumulative authorized model changes. Successful reconstruc-
tions match independently retained archived final IRs for seven states, before
adding the explicit routing-worker field absent from that schema. All 20 recon-
                         Candidate-Bound Evidence for RTL-to-GDS Signoff            5

structions are consistent with archived defaults and logged changes; indepen-
dent complete IR records for the other thirteen were not found in the inspected
archive. All 20 states are then run independently from RTL on the frozen con-
troller with one routing worker and further repair disabled. No new model calls
generate these states. The supplementary record retains the original episodes,
earlier measurements, reconstruction checks, and incomplete scale cases.
    The hypothetical no-recheck gate promotes every authorized state. We report
its failed-native-check fraction separately for each originating fixture class. The
proposal sequence was collected under native feedback, so these fractions describe
fixed states rather than the trajectory of an independently executed controller
without rechecking.


3.3   Scripted baseline without a model

A predeclared sweep starts from the initial configurations of the three recorded
fixtures. Spacing uses padding 0–3; PDN compares injected offset 50 µm with
the schema default 2 µm. At fixed 3 ns, the timing grid crosses synthesis strat-
egy DELAY0/DELAY1, density 0.45/0.55, padding 1/2, and native post-CTS
setup repair off/on with a 0.05 ns target margin. The initial timing fixture uses
AREA0 before these scripted changes. Two owner-declared 3.25/3.50 ns controls
retain that initial configuration and change the clock requirement. The 0.2 delay
fractions remain fixed, so absolute input/output delays scale from 0.60 ns to
0.65/0.70 ns. These controls are not an isolated clock-period sensitivity test or
repairs of the 3 ns target.
    All 24 states run from RTL with one routing worker and no model. A study-
only harness stops at the first failed native hard gate; successful states require the
frozen controller’s complete certification. Native checks, constraints within each
declared stratum, and verification decks are retained. The prior CLI pilot repeat-
edly rerouted unchanged IR because date-bearing GDS changed failed-input fin-
gerprints. The study harness bounds this execution without changing the frozen
controller. That pilot and the harness bootstrap failure remain excluded in the
artifact. The supplement records the stopping policy and full candidate metrics.

4.4   Explaining the narrow timing repair
The archived repaired ALU configuration passes at +1.586 ps under its recorded
source but fails at −0.654 ps under the frozen controller, at the same 3 ns period.
We test CTS-padding reapplication, routing-padding reapplication, and routing
workers in a 2 × 2 × 2 factorial ablation. Placement padding stays at one site;
clock, uncertainty, load, RTL, and PDK remain fixed. The supplement contains
the full eight-run matrix.
    Both worker counts give the same slack in every padding configuration. Reap-
plying CTS padding changes the archived-style result from +1.586 ps to −0.654
ps. Keeping routing padding while removing the CTS call gives +9.868 ps. Syn-
thesis netlist and placed DEF hashes match before the intervention; CTS DEF
and downstream artifacts differ, including legalized cell and hold-buffer posi-
tions. This isolates the CTS padding call’s effect in these executions. The frozen
controller retains that call and blocks the narrow configuration. The earlier pass
therefore belongs to its recorded snapshot and is not presented as a repair re-
producible on HEAD.

5      Related Work
OpenROAD and OpenLane provide implementation infrastructure [1,2]. MET-
RICS2.1 structures flow measurement and parameter tuning [4], and PROV
represents provenance relationships [5]. Nix supports fixed-output hashing and
experimental content-addressed derivations; Bazel maps action hashes to cached
results and stores outputs by content [6,7]. SLSA build provenance relates ar-
tifact digests to build definitions and builder identities [8]. These mechanisms
can carry domain-specific checks as declared actions and policies. This evalua-
tion specializes release acceptance to native EDA invocation, report structure,
approved inputs, and cached lineage; it does not evaluate Nix/Bazel execution
or SLSA conformance.
    ChatEDA studies model-driven EDA task planning, script generation, and
execution [9]. ORFS-agent optimizes OpenROAD-flow-scripts parameters [10];
MCP4EDA connects model interaction to RTL-to-GDS execution and backend
synthesis feedback [11]. FlowFixer-EDA uses log extraction, patch validation,
and native tool feedback for diagnosis and repair [12]. Its patch validation al-
ready demonstrates the importance of rechecking. Here, the specific object of
study is the release contract binding the selected GDS, approved verification
inputs, native report structure, and cached gate lineage. EDAid uses multiple
model-controlled agents for EDA workflow execution [13]. Ghose et al. study ex-
ecutable OpenROAD changes and measured quality of results [14]. Ghose et al.’s
newer ICCAD author manuscript also records patch/snapshot/proof manifests
                        Candidate-Bound Evidence for RTL-to-GDS Signoff         11

and renewed checks. Their manifest and rechecking mechanisms overlap with
generic manifest-based revalidation; we claim no priority for that mechanism.
    ChiBench supplies RTL designs for EDA-tool testing [15]. DRVerify docu-
ments physical-verification runset testing with known legal and violating struc-
tures [16]. That runset-testing rationale motivates checking rule execution and
intended coverage in addition to marker counts. The present contribution com-
bines constructed native/ledger faults, candidate lineage, fresh recorded-proposal
checks, and artifact-level repeat evidence. Comparable contracts in other con-
trollers are not excluded by this study.


6   Scope and Conclusion
The evidence establishes six-class coverage, class-specific proposal outcomes, and
exact repeat levels on one frozen implementation. All seven primary pairs repro-
duce their routed and date-normalized artifacts. The replay’s failures are con-
fined to the timing fixture, and the padding experiment explains why its earlier
marginal pass does not transfer to HEAD. The small synthetic circuits provide
no population repair rate or industrial-scale qualification. The local contract
uses a single typical corner and excludes multi-corner timing, on-chip variation,
power integrity, and foundry qualification. Hashing also assumes trusted tools,
controller, host, and PDK. Identity and approved inventory do not prove com-
pleteness of foundry rules or immunity to forged records.
    The nine live episodes remain an archived case study on 18d948815ac6;
their 7/9 certifications are not a frozen-source repair rate. The separate ex-
ploratory accumulator cohort certifies 0 of 4: two policy escalations and two
credit-interrupted episodes with unknown eventual outcomes. The largest clean
design is the synthetic 1,024-cell shift register. The supplement retains earlier
observations, failed-fingerprint proposals and incomplete scale probes. Logs re-
tain updates and final artifacts; rollback can overwrite intermediate files. The
regression record is 955 passed, 15 skipped, zero failures/errors.
